      What should happen

      Categories

        Removing a category always asks what to do with its expenses first — they are never left pointing at something that no longer exists. “Other” stays, because it is where anything unassigned lands.

        Delete all data

        Your categories and budgets stay. Your expenses do not, and there is no copy anywhere else. Export first if you might want them back.

        Monthly budgets

        Whole rupees, the same every month. Leave a category blank to leave it untracked — that is different from a budget of zero, which means you intend to spend nothing there.
