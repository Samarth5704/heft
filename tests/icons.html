Icon silhouette test

The reference app gives Cab/Auto, Car and Fuel three near-identical car glyphs, which is unreadable at a glance and useless to anyone who cannot separate the hues. This sheet renders the whole set desaturated at 40 px, rasterises each glyph, and compares every pair by overlap. Anything above the threshold is too similar in silhouette and needs redrawing — colour is not allowed to be what tells two icons apart.
